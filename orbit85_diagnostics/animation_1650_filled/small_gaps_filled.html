Tiny missing pixels: before and after filling

Frames 1650–1699. We fill only enclosed missing-radiance components with at most 16 cells and a bounding box no larger than 5 × 5 cells, entirely inside valid geolocation. Each estimated pixel is solved from its immediate neighbors (discrete harmonic interpolation). Valid measurements, larger masks and footprint boundaries remain unchanged.

The top row compares radiance; the bottom row compares matching texture using the same one-pixel smoothing and high-pass filter. Gray areas remain missing. Filling restores support around small holes, so the change in the texture footprint is larger than the number of estimated pixels. All color scales remain fixed through time. Loop reset is not physical motion.

Filled values are estimates, not new observations. They are saved with a separate interpolation mask. This is a separate animation experiment; the original source files and orbit products have not been replaced.
